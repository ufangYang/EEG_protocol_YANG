Study Protocol and Session Record Beta

This is a beta version, and the fields are still being developed with the electroencephalography and optically pumped magnetometer communities. Contributions are welcome. You can suggest a field, report a problem or propose a change through the issue page, or write to yu-fang.yang@fu-berlin.de.

For electroencephalography and optically pumped magnetometer magnetoencephalography. Part 1 declares the study, the planned recording and the planned preprocessing before any data are collected; lock it to add a timestamp. Part 2 records, at each session, what was actually done and where it differed from the plan. The form runs entirely in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.
